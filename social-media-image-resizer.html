Social media image resizing is the process of adjusting image dimensions to meet the specific requirements of different social media platforms. Each platform has optimal sizes for profile pictures, cover images, post images, stories, and more.

Platform-Specific Image Guide

📷

Instagram

Square, portrait, and landscape options. Stories are vertical. Reels use 1080×1920.

📘

Facebook

Wide landscape works best for feeds. Square images perform well too.

🐦

Twitter

Landscape orientation is standard. Vertical images may be cropped.

💼

LinkedIn

Professional landscape images perform best. Avoid excessive text.

📌

Pinterest

Vertical pins get the most engagement. Use tall aspect ratios.

▶️

YouTube

Thumbnails should be high-contrast and readable at small sizes.

Understanding Aspect Ratios

1:1

Perfect square. Ideal for Instagram posts and profile pictures.

4:5

Tall portrait. Popular for Instagram and Pinterest.

16:9

Wide landscape. Standard for YouTube, Twitter, and videos.

9:16

Vertical portrait. Perfect for stories, Reels, and TikTok.

1.91:1

Facebook and LinkedIn standard post format.

2:3

Pinterest's preferred aspect ratio for pins.

21:9

Ultrawide for cinematic shots and some social media.

5:4

Classic photo format, good for prints and some platforms.

Social Media Image Best Practices

  • Always use high-quality source images
  • Keep important content in the center of the image
  • Test images on mobile devices before posting
  • Use consistent branding across all platforms
  • Optimize file size without sacrificing quality
  • Use platform-specific dimensions for best results
  • Consider the layout of different platforms
  • Use high contrast for readability on thumbnails
  • Maintain consistent colors and style
  • Preview images in context before publishing
