What Is PDF Image Extraction?

PDF image extraction is the process of identifying and extracting image data from PDF documents and saving them as individual image files. This allows you to reuse images without having to recreate them.

  • Multiple Formats — JPG or PNG output

Extract vs Export: What's the Difference?

🖼️ Extract Images

Extracts images from the PDF and saves them as separate image files. Preserves the original image data and quality.

📄 Export PDF to Image

Converts entire PDF pages into images. This creates images of whole pages, not individual images within the document.

Use Extract to get individual images from a PDF. Use Export to convert whole pages into images. Extract is more selective, while Export captures entire pages.
