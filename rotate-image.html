An image rotator is a tool that allows you to change the orientation of an image by rotating it to any angle or flipping it horizontally or vertically. Whether you need to fix a sideways photo, create a mirrored effect, or adjust composition, our tool makes it easy.

Our free online image rotator lets you rotate images by 90°, 180°, 270°, or any custom angle. You can also flip images horizontally or vertically. Perfect for photographers, designers, and anyone who needs to fix image orientation quickly.

What Is Image Rotation?

Image rotation is the process of turning an image around its center point to change its orientation. This can be done at fixed angles (90°, 180°, 270°) or custom angles (any degree between 0° and 360°).

Key aspects of image rotation include:

  • Clockwise Rotation — Rotate right by 90°, 180°, or 270°
  • Counter-Clockwise Rotation — Rotate left by 90°, 180°, or 270°
  • Custom Angles — Rotate to any degree
  • Horizontal Flip — Mirror the image left to right
  • Vertical Flip — Mirror the image top to bottom

Rotation vs Flip

🔄 Rotation

Rotates the image around its center at any angle (0-360°). Changes the orientation but preserves the image content's layout.

🪞 Flip / Mirror

Creates a mirror image by reversing the image horizontally or vertically. Does not change the angle but reverses the image.

Rotation changes the angle, while flipping creates a mirror image. Both are useful for different purposes. Use rotation to fix tilted photos and flipping for creative mirrored effects.

Image Rotation Best Practices

  • Always keep a backup of your original image
  • Use EXIF data for auto-rotation when possible
  • Check the image after rotation for any quality loss
  • Consider the composition after rotation
  • Use 90° increments for standard orientations
  • Use custom angles for creative effects
  • Flip images for mirrored creative compositions
  • Test images across different viewing platforms
  • Save in lossless formats if editing repeatedly
  • Preview before final download
