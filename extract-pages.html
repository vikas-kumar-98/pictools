What Is PDF Page Extraction?

PDF page extraction is the process of selecting specific pages from a PDF document and saving them as separate PDF files. This allows you to isolate important pages, remove unwanted content, or create new documents from selected pages.

Key features include:

  • Multiple Pages — Select multiple non-consecutive pages

Extract vs Split: What's the Difference?

📌 Extract Pages

Select specific pages to create new PDF files. You choose exactly which pages to extract, skipping unwanted pages entirely.

✂️ Split PDF

Divide the entire PDF into sections (e.g., pages 1-5, 6-10). All pages are included, just separated into groups.

Use Extract when you only want specific pages. Use Split when you want to divide the entire document into parts. Extract is more selective, while Split is more comprehensive.
