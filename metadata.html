Free Online Image Metadata Viewer

Image metadata is hidden information stored within image files that provides details about the image, including when and where it was taken, camera settings, and more. Also known as EXIF data, this information can be invaluable for photographers, content creators, and digital forensics.

Our free online metadata viewer allows you to extract and view EXIF data from your images instantly. Upload any image to see camera settings, GPS coordinates, date taken, and other hidden details. You can also remove sensitive metadata for privacy protection.

What Is Image Metadata?

Image metadata is embedded data that describes various aspects of an image file. It's automatically recorded by cameras and smartphones when a photo is taken.

Common types of metadata include:

  • EXIF Data — Camera settings, date, time
  • GPS Coordinates — Location where the photo was taken
  • Camera Information — Make, model, and lens details
  • Image Properties — Resolution, color space, bit depth
  • Copyright Information — Creator and rights management
