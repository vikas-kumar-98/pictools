PDF to Word conversion is the process of extracting text, images, and formatting from a PDF document and creating an editable Word (.docx) file. This allows you to edit, reuse, and collaborate on the content.

PDF vs Word: Which to Use?

📄 PDF

Best for sharing and printing. Preserves formatting across all devices. Ideal for final versions and official documents.

📝 Word

Best for editing and collaboration. Easy to update, format, and work with others. Ideal for drafting and revisions.

Use PDF for final versions and sharing. Use Word for editing, collaboration, and content creation.
