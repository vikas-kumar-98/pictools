Our story

We started with a simple belief: online tools should be fast, private, and genuinely helpful. No ads, no tracking, no server uploads — just pure client-side processing that respects your data.

Today, our platform serves thousands of users who rely on our image optimizer, PDF tools, and developer utilities. We’re a small, independent team passionate about building software that feels premium and works reliably.

100% browser-based — your files never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to.

What we build

Our core tools are designed for efficiency and precision:

  • Bulk Image Optimizer — Compress JPG, PNG, WEBP in batch, with quality control and real-time previews.
  • Image converter — Switch formats without losing quality.
  • PDF utilities — Merge, split, and compress PDFs locally.
  • Developer tools — JSON formatter, code minifiers, and more.

We continuously improve our tools based on user feedback. If you have a feature request, we’d love to hear it.

Privacy & security

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Here’s how we protect it:

  • Local processing: Images and files are processed in your browser. We don’t upload them to any server (unless you explicitly save or share).
  • No persistent storage: We don’t permanently store your files. Temporary data is cleared when you close the page.
  • Minimal analytics: We only collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the service. No personal identifiers.
  • Secure connections: All communications are encrypted with HTTPS.

We don’t sell, share, or rent your data. Ever. Our business model is simple: we offer premium tools and keep them sustainable through optional pro features.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
