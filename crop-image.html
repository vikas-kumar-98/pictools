An image cropper is a tool that allows you to remove unwanted parts of an image and select a specific area to keep. Whether you need to resize for social media, improve composition, or focus on a specific subject, cropping is an essential editing technique.

Our free online image cropper allows you to upload any image and crop it to any size or aspect ratio. Perfect for social media, design projects, and photography. No registration, no watermarks, and no limits.

What Is Image Cropping?

Image cropping is the process of removing unwanted outer areas of an image to improve framing, composition, or focus on a specific subject. It allows you to change the aspect ratio and remove distracting elements.

Key features include:

  • Remove Unwanted Areas — Focus on the subject
  • Change Aspect Ratio — Fit specific dimensions
  • Improve Composition — Apply the rule of thirds
  • Multiple Formats — JPG, PNG, and more
  • Quick Processing — Instant results
