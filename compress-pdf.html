A PDF compressor is a tool that reduces the file size of PDF documents while maintaining acceptable quality. Whether you need to share files via email, upload to websites, or save storage space, our tool makes it quick and easy.

Our free online PDF compressor allows you to compress PDFs with different quality levels — from high compression for maximum size reduction to minimal compression for preserving quality. No registration, no watermarks, and no limits.

What Is PDF Compression?

PDF compression is the process of reducing the file size of a PDF document by optimizing images, fonts, and other elements. This makes files easier to share, upload, and store.

Key aspects include:

  • Image Optimization — Compress images within the PDF
  • Font Subsetting — Include only used fonts
  • Remove Redundancy — Eliminate duplicate data
  • Quality Control — Balance size and quality
  • Multiple Compression Levels — Choose your preference

Compression Levels Explained

📊

High Compression

Reduces file size by 70-90%. Best for email attachments and web sharing. Quality may be reduced for images.

⚖️

Medium Compression

Reduces file size by 40-60%. Good balance for most use cases. Maintains reasonable quality.

🎨

Low Compression

Reduces file size by 15-30%. Preserves maximum quality. Ideal for print and archival purposes.

Quality vs Size Trade-off

📊 High Compression

Smallest file size, some quality loss. Best for: Email attachments, web uploads, quick sharing.

⚖️ Medium Compression

Good balance, minimal quality loss. Best for: Documents, presentations, everyday use.

🎨 Low Compression

Largest file size, maximum quality. Best for: Printing, portfolios, legal documents.

Choose the compression level based on your needs. High compression saves space, while low compression preserves quality. Medium compression offers the best of both worlds.

PDF Compression Best Practices

  • Choose the right compression level for your needs
  • Check the quality after compression

  • Optimize images before creating the PDF
  • Consider using medium compression for documents
