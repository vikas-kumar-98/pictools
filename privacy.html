Information We Collect

We may collect information that is necessary for operating and improving the website. This includes:

  • Voluntarily provided information (e.g., when you contact us).
  • Contact information such as email address when you reach out.
  • Basic technical information including browser and device details.
  • IP address and usage information (e.g., pages visited, features used).
  • Error and performance data to help us improve reliability.

We collect only what is reasonably needed to provide and enhance our service.

File & Tool Processing

How we handle your files

For tools such as image compressors, PDF tools, converters, and generators:

  • When possible, files are processed locally in your browser, meaning they never leave your device.
  • Files are temporarily stored only as required for processing and are not retained longer than necessary.
  • We do not permanently store uploaded files unless explicitly required for the tool’s functionality.
  • Please avoid uploading sensitive or confidential information when using our tools.

Processing practices are designed to respect your privacy and limit data exposure.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Providing website functionality and processing requested tools.
  • Improving performance, fixing bugs, and preventing abuse.
  • Understanding usage trends to enhance user experience.
  • Responding to support requests and communications.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve our website.

  • Essential cookies: Required for core functionality (e.g., security, session management).
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and choices.
  • Optional tracking technologies: Used only with your consent where required.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party services for various aspects of our operations, including:

  • Analytics, hosting, security, payments, advertising, and infrastructure.
  • These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not use third-party services unless they are necessary for the operation of our platform.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Secure connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access controls and infrastructure security.
  • Abuse prevention and regular maintenance.

While we strive to protect your information, no system is completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including:

  • Temporary file-processing data: deleted shortly after processing.
  • Account-related information (if applicable): retained for the duration of your account.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregated form for a limited period.
  • Support communications: kept as needed to resolve inquiries.

Data Sharing

We may share information in limited circumstances, such as:

  • With service providers who assist in operating our website.
  • To comply with legal requirements or security investigations.
  • In the event of a business transfer (merger, acquisition).
  • With your consent, where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access – request a copy of your data.
  • Correction – update inaccurate information.
  • Deletion – request removal of your data.
  • Portability – receive your data in a structured format.
  • Restriction / Objection – limit certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent – where processing is based on consent.
  • Cookie preferences – manage tracking technologies.

Available rights may vary based on applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete it.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own. When we transfer data across borders, we take appropriate safeguards to protect your privacy.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The “Last Updated” date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Significant changes may be communicated through website notices.
