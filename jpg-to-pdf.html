JPG vs Other Formats for PDF

🖼️ JPG to PDF

Best for photographs and complex images. JPG offers good compression with minimal quality loss for photos. Ideal for portfolios and image collections.

📄 PNG to PDF

Best for graphics with transparency or text. PNG is lossless and preserves sharp edges. Better for logos, screenshots, and illustrations.

JPG is the most common format for photos and web images. Converting JPG to PDF is ideal for creating photo portfolios, product catalogs, and image-based documents.
